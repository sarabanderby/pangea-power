pangea-api deployed.

1. Wait for it to come up:

     oc rollout status deployment/pangea-api

2. Check health (expects the pangea-db Service to be reachable):

     oc exec deployment/pangea-api -- curl -s localhost:8080/api/health

3. External URL for the dashboard:

     echo "https://$(oc get route pangea-api -o jsonpath='{.spec.host}')/api/fleet/status"

In-cluster endpoint: pangea-api:8080
DB credentials come from Secret pangea-db-credentials.
